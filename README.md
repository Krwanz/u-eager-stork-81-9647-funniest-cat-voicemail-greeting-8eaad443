An entry for the [Funniest Cat Voicemail Greeting](https://make.fun/c/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-cat-voicemail-greeting-8eaad443) bounty on make.fun.

## The entry

Mochi the cat records his own voicemail greeting — too busy knocking the
caller's mug off the table to take the call, and happy to accept tuna at
4 a.m. instead of a message:

> Hi, you've reached Mochi. I'm knocking your mug off the table. Leave a message after the beep, or bring tuna at 4 a.m.

One line, 118 characters, inside the 120-character limit.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the greeting to `/output/entry.txt` (or to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that is set):

```sh
python3 main.py
```
